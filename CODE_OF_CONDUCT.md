# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes empathy and kindness, respect for different viewpoints and experiences, constructive feedback, accepting responsibility and focusing on the community's interests.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing another person's private information without permission, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibility

Dmitrii Erusov, as project maintainer, or the maintainer's successor as owner of the project, is the enforcement authority and may appoint additional maintainers in writing. The enforcement authority may clarify these standards and take proportionate corrective action, including editing or removing comments and rejecting future contributions.

Moderation, suspension or exclusion affects participation in project spaces only. It does not revoke copyright or patent licenses already granted under an executed Contributor License Agreement, nor does it alter licenses already granted by the project for distributed software.

This Code of Conduct is a published community policy, not a promise that every complaint will produce a particular outcome or timetable and not a waiver of lawful rights.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when a person officially represents the project in public spaces, including through an official email address, social-media account or appointed role.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `devs@veriqa.app`. Do not use the security-reporting channel for ordinary conduct complaints unless the complaint also concerns a vulnerability or immediate security risk.

Reports will be reviewed promptly, impartially and on a need-to-know basis. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed: information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to investigate, protect participants, obtain advice, comply with law or provide procedural fairness. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.

Possible consequences, depending on context and impact, include:

1. **Correction** — a private explanation or request to change behavior.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning and temporary interaction restrictions.
3. **Temporary ban** — exclusion from project interaction for a stated period.
4. **Permanent ban** — indefinite exclusion from public project interaction.

The enforcement authority may adapt these measures where necessary for safety, fairness or applicable law.

## Personal data

Complaint information is used to assess and enforce this policy, protect participants and establish or defend legal claims. Access is limited to those who need it. Information may be stored or accessed where the maintainer and relevant service providers operate. Submit only information reasonably necessary for the complaint. Privacy-related requests may be sent to `devs@veriqa.app`.
