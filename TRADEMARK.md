  names you first and makes the relationship clear — `Acme.Veriqa.Slack`,

- Publish packages under the `Veriqa.*` NuGet identifier prefix or images

## Forks

The license lets you fork the code, change it, and redistribute it. The
policy asks one thing: **ship the fork under your own name**, without the
logo. You may — and should — say that it is derived from Veriqa. A fork that
keeps the "Powered by Veriqa" attribution unmodified may keep it; a fork that
removes it must not keep the logo anywhere else.

## Why a policy and not just the license

The license protects the code; the marks protect users from confusion about
what they are running and who stands behind it. Keeping the name with the
original project is what lets an integrator tell an official release from a
derivative, and what makes "Powered by Veriqa" mean something.
